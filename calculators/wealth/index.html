Mutual Fund SIP

Compounding Mechanics in India

Selecting the right vehicle depends on your liquidity requirements, tax bracket, and horizon. Equity Mutual Funds (SIP/Lumpsum) offer higher CAGR potential with long-term capital gains (LTCG) taxed at 12.5% above ₹1.25 Lakhs under Section 112A.

Fixed-income products like PPF provide tax-free EEE returns at 7.1%, while Bank FDs and RDs compound quarterly with interest added to taxable income under slab rates. NPS and APY provide structured retirement income streams for long-term security.
