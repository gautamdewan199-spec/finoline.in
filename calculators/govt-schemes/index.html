Key Differences: PMEGP vs. MUDRA Yojana

PMEGP (KVIC): A credit-linked capital subsidy scheme meant exclusively for new greenfield micro-enterprises. The Ministry of MSME provides a non-repayable Margin Money grant of 15% to 35%, locked in a 3-year term deposit before adjusting against your bank loan principal. Mandatory EDP training is required.

PMMY Mudra Loans: Offers collateral-free institutional credit up to ₹20 Lakhs across 4 categories: Shishu (Up to ₹50k), Kishore (₹50k to ₹5L), Tarun (₹5L to ₹10L), and Tarun Plus (₹10L to ₹20L for proven track records). Mudra provides direct working capital and term loans without a government capital subsidy.
