How Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) Compounding Works

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) automates wealth creation by investing a fixed sum into equity or hybrid mutual funds every month, regardless of market volatility. When stock prices dip, your fixed rupee installment acquires more mutual fund units; when markets rally, your existing units appreciate—a mechanism known as rupee-cost averaging.

Pairing a disciplined monthly SIP with an annual Step-Up (Top-Up) is the single most reliable method for salaried Indian professionals to outpace lifestyle inflation. Increasing your SIP by just 10% every year after annual appraisals dramatically compresses the time required to reach multi-crore financial independence milestones.

Frequently Asked Questions (Mutual Fund SIP India)

1. How is Mutual Fund SIP return calculated in India?

SIP returns are calculated using the compound future value of an annuity due: FV = P × [((1 + r)^n - 1) / r] × (1 + r), accounting for monthly compounding on every installment from the date of debit.

2. What is a Step-Up SIP and how much extra wealth does it generate?

A Step-Up SIP automatically increases your monthly contribution by a fixed percentage (e.g., 10%) every 12 months. Over 20 years at 12% CAGR, a 10% annual step-up nearly doubles your final maturity corpus compared to keeping your SIP constant.

3. How much monthly SIP is needed to reach ₹1 Crore in 15 years?

Assuming a 12% annualized return, a monthly SIP of ₹20,000 builds approximately ₹1.01 Crore over 15 years on a total principal outgo of ₹36 Lakh.
