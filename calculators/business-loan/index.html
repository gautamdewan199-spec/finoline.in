Difference Between Business Term Loans, CC, and Overdraft

Business Term Loans: Disbursed as a one-time lump sum with fixed monthly EMIs covering both principal and interest over a structured tenure (1 to 10 years).

Cash Credit (CC) & Overdraft (OD): Revolving credit lines where interest is calculated strictly on the daily utilized balance rather than the total sanctioned limit. Interest is debited monthly, making CC/OD facilities ideal for managing inventory, receivables, and operational cash flow cycles.
