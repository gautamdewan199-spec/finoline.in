New vs. Old Tax Regime Rules

New Tax Regime (Default): Provides lower slab rates and a higher Section 87A tax rebate for taxable incomes up to ₹12 Lakhs (rendering salaried income up to ₹12.75 Lakhs tax-free with the ₹75,000 Standard Deduction). However, it forfeits major deductions under 80C, 80D, and HRA.

Old Tax Regime: Keeps higher traditional slab rates (up to 30% above ₹10 Lakhs) but permits tax savings through extensive deductions (Section 80C up to ₹1.5L, 80D, HRA, Section 24 Home Loan Interest, and NPS).
