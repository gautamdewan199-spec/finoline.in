How Home Loan Prepayment Saves Lakhs

In the early years of a 20 or 30-year home loan, over 70% of your EMI goes toward bank interest while only a fraction pays down the principal balance. Adding even a modest extra monthly payment reduces the principal directly, cutting off compounding interest for all remaining years.
