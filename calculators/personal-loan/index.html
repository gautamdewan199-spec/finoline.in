RBI Rules on Personal Loan Prepayment Penalties

Under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) directions, regulated lenders are prohibited from charging foreclosure or prepayment penalties on floating-rate personal loans granted to individual non-business borrowers.

However, most digital and retail personal loans carry fixed interest rates. For fixed-rate loans, lenders may charge foreclosure fees if explicitly disclosed in your Key Fact Statement (KFS). Prepayment penalties attract an additional 18% GST in India.
