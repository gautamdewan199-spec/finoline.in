Curriculum & Roadmap

The 12-Chapter Blueprint

Sequential execution stages taking you from basic financial hygiene to advanced portfolio derisking.

CH 01 Foundation

Diagnosing Your Financial Hygiene

Auditing net worth, calculating personal savings rates, identifying hidden debt traps, and setting up clean accounting buffers.

CH 02 Safety Buffer

Emergency Runway Engineering

Constructing 6–12 months of liquidity using high-yield savings accounts, sweep-in FDs, and liquid mutual funds for immediate access.

CH 03 Risk Shield

Insurance: Health & Pure Term Life

Decoupling investment from insurance. Evaluating sum assured calculations, room rent sub-limits, super top-ups, and rider exclusions.

CH 04 Debt Payoff

Crushing High-Interest Debt

Deploying Mathematical Avalanche vs. Behavioral Snowball techniques to systematically eliminate personal loans and credit cards.

CH 05 Asset Allocation

Demystifying Asset Classes

Understanding real returns across Domestic Equity, Fixed Income, Gold, International Equities, and Real Estate over 20-year horizons.

CH 06 Equity Core

The Passive Indexing Advantage

Why 85%+ of active fund managers underperform the benchmark. Choosing Nifty 50, Nifty Next 50, and total market index funds.

CH 07 Debt Engine

Fixed Income & Capital Preservation

Selecting Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB), Public Provident Fund (PPF), Target Maturity Debt Funds, and RBI Floating Rate Savings Bonds.

CH 08 Tax Mechanics

Tax Optimization & Section 112A

Navigating the 12.5% LTCG regime. Executing annual ₹1.25 Lakh tax harvesting strategies legally without interrupting compounding.

CH 09 Execution

Designing Your Investment Policy Statement

Formulating an institutional IPS mandate specifying asset split targets, step-up SIP percentages, and explicit rebalancing rules.

CH 10 Maintenance

Automating Portfolio Rebalancing

Executing corridor-based vs. calendar-based rebalancing to systematically sell high and buy low without emotional interference.

CH 11 Decumulation

Retirement Decumulation & SWP

Transitioning from accumulation to distribution. Engineering bucket strategies and Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP) for pension cash flows.

CH 12 Psychology

Behavioral Discipline During Market Crashes

Mitigating recency bias, loss aversion, and FOMO. Managing drawdowns during 30%+ market corrections without panic selling.
