How the Institutional Robo-Advisory Engine Designs Your Portfolio

Most retail mutual fund calculators show misleadingly rosy numbers because they assume 100% equity returns every single year, ignore inflation, and omit capital gains taxes. The Finans.in Institutional Robo-Advisory Architect solves all three blind spots by applying institutional portfolio engineering principles to Indian DIY investing:

  • Core-Satellite Mutual Fund Construction: Anchors 30% to 60% of your capital in low-cost Nifty 50 Index funds (Core) while deploying 10% to 25% into Nifty Next 50 and Midcap indices (Satellite) to capture alpha without reckless concentration.
  • Automated 36-Month Glidepath Derisking: Prevents sequence-of-returns risk by gradually shifting equity exposure into Banking & PSU debt and arbitrage funds during the final three years before your target financial goal.
  • Real Purchasing Power & LTCG Tax Drag: Discounts your nominal maturity corpus by 6% annual CPI inflation and deducts Section 112A Long-Term Capital Gains tax (12.5% above the ₹1.25 lakh annual exemption) so you see exact spendable wealth.

Frequently Asked Questions (Robo-Advisory & Portfolio Planning)

1. How does an AI Robo Advisor build a mutual fund asset allocation in India?

It evaluates your behavioral crash tolerance alongside your investment horizon, splitting capital across Nifty 50 Index funds, Nifty Next 50/Midcap funds, Banking & PSU debt, and Sovereign Gold—then generates a downloadable Investment Policy Statement (IPS) so you can execute via zero-commission direct mutual funds.

2. What is Glidepath Capital Preservation in goal-based investing?

Glidepath derisking automatically reduces equity exposure during the final 36 months of your timeline, moving accumulated profits into short-duration debt so a sudden market downturn right before retirement or a major expense cannot wipe out years of compounding.

3. Why does this planner show both Nominal Value and Real Purchasing Power?

At 6% annual Indian retail inflation, ₹1 Crore 15 years from today has the purchasing power of roughly ₹41.7 Lakh in today’s money. Showing inflation-adjusted real value alongside post-tax LTCG deductions prevents under-saving for long-term financial independence.
